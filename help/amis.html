EC2 Images
This page lists all of the EC2 images that are available for use when creating new EC2 virtual systems. It includes both those from Amazon, Virtualmin, or other EC2 accounts that you have selected to view public images from.

For images that you have created, the AMI ID can be clicked on to display additional information and to control exactly which EC2 accounts have access to it. You can also use the checkboxes next to your image and the Delete, Public and Private buttons at the bottom of the page to remove AMIs, grant them to other EC2 accounts, or limit them only to specific accounts.
