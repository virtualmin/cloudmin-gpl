Backup Virtual Machines

Select the machines and a destination, then choose how to prepare running machines and whether to compress the archives. Each machine gets a separate full backup. Follow its progress from Active Jobs.

Backups include disks, attached media, machine settings, UEFI variables and Cloudmin's managed SSH access. Guest passwords and installed license credentials are included too. Archives are not encrypted by Cloudmin; keep the destination private.

For scheduled backups, enter a description, choose a schedule and set retention. Disabling a schedule keeps its settings and existing backups.

Full backups need temporary copies as well as the final archive. Allow roughly four times the copied disks' allocated size on the staging filesystem, plus source-pool copies and the destination archive. If they share a filesystem, allow space for all of them there. LVM staging checks use the full disk capacity, even when the temporary copy is sparse. Raw disks can also need their full logical size. Live backups need additional room for temporary snapshots.

Managed raw and QCOW2 files, LVM disks and LVM-thin disks are supported. Unmanaged host devices, TPMs and other external device state are not.
