Backup Virtual Machines

Each selected machine gets a full backup of its disks, settings and credentials. Backups run in the background; you can leave the page and follow them in Active Jobs.

Choose guest-agent preparation to keep machines running, or a clean shutdown for applications that need it. Already stopped machines stay stopped. Backups do not include RAM, snapshot history or external device state.

Archives are not encrypted by Cloudmin. Keep the destination private and allow local space for temporary disk copies and the archive, even for remote backups.
