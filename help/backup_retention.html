Delete backups older than

Disabled keeps all backups. Otherwise choose an age from the Days, Weeks, Months or Years group. Retention uses elapsed time: a week is 7 days, a month is 30 days and a year is 365 days.

Deletion runs only after every selected machine has been backed up successfully by this schedule. It applies only to this schedule's recorded archives, and always keeps the newest successful backup of each machine even when it is older than the selected age. Manual backups and other schedules' backups are left alone.

The destination account needs permission to delete archives. Disabling retention or the schedule does not recover archives that have already been deleted.
