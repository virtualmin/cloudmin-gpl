Backup and restore space requirements

A full backup needs space for source-pool disk copies, worker staging and the final archive. A remote destination does not remove the local space requirement.

As a planning estimate, allow roughly four times the copied disks' allocated size on the staging filesystem, plus the source-pool copies and destination archive where those use separate filesystems. If they share a filesystem, allow space for all of them there. Sparse files and reflinks can reduce actual use, but do not assume they are available.

File pools and standard LVM pools need room for full disk copies and temporary snapshot growth during live backups. Thin copies and snapshots share their pool's data and metadata space. LVM staging checks also require free space for the full disk capacity on the host, even when the temporary copy is sparse. Raw disks and extracted archives can require their full logical size. A small compressed archive can therefore need much more space to restore.

Restore first downloads and checks the archive in local staging. Importing it needs further space to extract and inspect the disks, plus space in the selected storage pool for their full capacities. Do not size the pool from the compressed archive size alone.

Cloudmin checks space during the job. Other activity can still consume it, so leave headroom. Temporary inspection downloads are removed after restore; unused inspections expire after one day and are cleaned up hourly or when the backup list is read.
