Backup and restore space requirements

Remote storage does not remove the need for local space. A backup needs disk copies, temporary working space and the final archive.

Allow roughly four times the copied disks' allocated size for staging, plus source-pool copies and the destination archive. Add these requirements when they share a filesystem. Live backups also need room for snapshot growth.

LVM staging checks use full disk capacity, even for sparse copies. Raw disks and extracted archives can also need their full capacity. Monitor both data and metadata space in thin pools.

Restore needs space to download, extract and inspect the archive, plus room in the destination pool for the disks. A small compressed archive can require much more space to restore. Leave headroom for other activity.
