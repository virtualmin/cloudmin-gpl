Backups and Restore

This page lists completed backups and saved schedules. Each archive contains a full virtual machine, including its disks, settings and credentials. Store archives privately; Cloudmin does not encrypt them.

  • Back Up Now creates a separate archive for each selected machine.
  • Add a Scheduled Backup sets an hourly, daily, weekly, monthly or yearly run. Use retention to delete older archives after successful backups.
  • Restore a Virtual Machine reads an archive and asks you to confirm before creating a stopped replacement with the original identity.

Use Restore beside a saved archive to reuse its recorded source settings. Delete backup removes that archive from its destination and cannot be undone. Click a schedule's description to change, disable or delete it; deleting a schedule does not delete its archives.

Click a field label in the backup or restore form for help with that option. Allow local space for disk copies, temporary work and the final archive, even when using a remote destination.
