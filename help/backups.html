Backups and Restore

A backup preserves a complete virtual machine for recovery. Archives include guest credentials and are not encrypted by Cloudmin, so store them privately.

Backups run in the background and can be scheduled. Retention deletes older archives after successful scheduled runs. Deleting a schedule keeps its archives; deleting an archive permanently removes it.

Restore preserves the original identity and creates a stopped machine. Keep the original off before starting the restored copy. Both backup and restore need local working space, even with remote storage.
