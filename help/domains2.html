Virtualmin Domains
This page lists Virtualmin domains that are currently hosted on this system. To manage a domain, click on its name in the list. Or to perform operations on multiple domains at once (such as deleting, disabling, enabling or moving to a new system), select the checkboxes next to them and click the appropriate button at the bottom of the table.

A domain can also be created specifically on this system using the Create Virtualmin domain link above or below the table.
