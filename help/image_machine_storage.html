Storage pool

Use an active directory, filesystem or NFS pool with room for the source disk's full capacity. The image is independent of the source.

Live capture also needs space for writes during copying: on the source filesystem for file disks, or in the volume group or thin pool for LVM. Temporary preparation needs further space on the host. Add these requirements when they share storage.

Capturing does not shrink the disk. New machines need at least its original capacity.
