License Manager

Recheck after renewing a license or fixing a connection problem. A replacement serial and key are validated before the saved license changes.

Expired licenses have a seven-day grace period. Excess host use allows 21 days to finish a migration or assign separate licenses. Rechecking does not restart these periods. Afterward, new changes are blocked, but running machines, accepted jobs and scheduled backups continue.

If the license server cannot be reached, a previously verified license can remain usable for up to 21 days, subject to its other deadlines.

Deregistering removes this host's saved license and repository credentials. It does not cancel your subscription or immediately reduce the server's host count.
