Networking

NAT gives guests outgoing access through the host; incoming connections need port forwarding. Bridging joins the upstream network directly. Routed networks need an upstream route through this host. Isolated networks do not forward traffic beyond the host.

Creating a host bridge moves the Ethernet interface's IP, gateway and DNS settings onto it and may disconnect you. Reconnect and confirm before the timer expires, or Cloudmin restores the old settings. Removing an unused bridge moves those settings back.

Detach all machines, including stopped ones, before removing a bridge. Custom interfaces, VLANs and macvlans may need Webmin Network Configuration. Changing a guest's network attachment does not update its OS configuration.

If cloud-init rewrites the host's network configuration at boot, update or disable that behavior before bridge conversion, or it may undo the change.
