Notifications

Notifications send email, chat and push messages or webhooks for resource use, guest status, monitoring problems and failed jobs. Checks run every 60 seconds by default. Missing readings do not count as recovery. A stopped host or Webmin service cannot send alerts.

Failed deliveries, including test messages, retry automatically with up to six attempts in total. Permanent Pushover rejections stop immediately. Correct the destination or restore its message quota before retrying failed deliveries. Success means the receiving server accepted the message, not that it reached an inbox. Retries can produce duplicates.

Disabling a rule or destination cancels its queued deliveries. Enabling a rule starts a new check period.

Muting pauses alerts for one guest or for the host and all guests. Muting all alerts also covers guests added later. Mutes stay active until you unmute them, even after restarts. Unmuting all alerts leaves separately muted guests muted. Unmuting one guest does not override a mute for all alerts. Changing a mute needs host and management access and is blocked in read-only mode.

Checks and history continue while muted. Pending deliveries are marked muted and will not be sent later. After unmuting, a problem still present at the next fresh check sends one new alert, even when reminders are disabled. A problem that has recovered stays quiet. Destination tests still send. A delivery already in progress may finish.

Alerts, recoveries, reminders and test results are kept for 30 days. Pending deliveries remain until they finish or expire.
