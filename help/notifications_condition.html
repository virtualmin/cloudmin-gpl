Condition

Resource conditions compare usage, free memory or space, host load, or network and disk rates with a threshold. They alert after the condition lasts for the configured duration. Resource collection is required except for host filesystem checks. Saving statistics history is optional.

Status conditions detect stopped guests, unavailable guest agents, inactive storage pools or a collector that stops providing fresh readings. Agent checks apply to running guests and may retry an hour apart. Collector alerts pause when collection is deliberately disabled. Job failures skip the duration wait. Planned guest stops and unfinished guest jobs pause guest checks.
