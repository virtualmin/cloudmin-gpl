Mount point or pool

For host filesystems, enter the absolute path of a mount point, such as / or /boot. Leaving it blank monitors /. A directory that is not a mount point gets no readings, so its rule never alerts.

For guest filesystems, enter a mount point or leave it blank to check every reported filesystem. For storage pools, enter a pool name or UUID, or leave it blank to check every active pool for usage or free space, or every known pool for inactivity. Other conditions ignore this field.
