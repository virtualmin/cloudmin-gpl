Duration in minutes

A condition must persist for this many minutes before an alert is recorded. The default is 5 minutes. Enter zero or more minutes. Zero allows an alert at the next check, once the minimum time between new alerts has also passed. Checks run at the notification check interval from the module configuration.

Missing readings or paused checks restart the waiting period and do not count as recovery. A condition that clears before its first alert leaves no alert or recovery event in history. Job failures ignore this setting.
