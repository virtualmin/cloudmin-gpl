Send recovery notifications

Enabling recovery notifications sends a message when fresh readings confirm that a condition has cleared after an alert. This is enabled by default. Missing readings do not count as recovery.

Numeric conditions must pass the threshold by two percentage points for usage, or 2% of the threshold for other measurements. A zero threshold clears when its comparison stops matching. Status conditions recover when the guest, agent, pool or collector returns to its expected state. Disabling recovery messages keeps alerts and reminders. Job failures have no recovery message.
