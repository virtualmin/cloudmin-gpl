Threshold

The units follow the condition: percent for usage, GiB for free memory or space, MiB/s for network and disk rates, and load average for host load. Usage thresholds accept 0.1 to 100. Other thresholds accept zero or more. Switching units resets the threshold and comparison to their defaults.

Defaults are 90% for usage, 1 GiB for free memory or space, 100 MiB/s for rates and 1 for load. Free memory and space rules default to alerting at or below the threshold. Other numeric conditions default to at or above. Status and job conditions do not use a threshold.
