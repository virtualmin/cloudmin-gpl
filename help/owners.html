System Owners
Cloudmin system owners are additional logins that can be restricted to managing one or more systems. Typically they are useful for customers who own virtual systems to view their status, upgrade Virtualmin-related packages, and shutdown or reboot them.

Each system owner can also be limited to performing only certain actions, such as running commands, upgrading scripts or shutting systems down. Most Cloudmin operations are not available to owners, in particular those that deal with global configuration or the management of host systems. In addition, system- level operations related to bandwidth, CPU and disk management cannot be performed.
