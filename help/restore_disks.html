Disks and attached media

This is the number of disks and attached media recorded in the archive, including seed or installer media when present. Restore imports them together; all listed disks are restored.

Disks are restored as independent volumes in the chosen storage pool, using raw logical volumes for LVM. Their guest-visible device names and contents are preserved. UEFI variables and Cloudmin's managed SSH key are restored separately when included in the archive.

A full backup preserves the disk contents at backup time, not RAM or the source machine's snapshot history.
