Archive filename

Enter the Cloudmin archive's filename, ending in .tar or .tar.gz. Do not include its directory, a URL or wildcards. Set the directory or cloud folder in Backup source.

Cloudmin-generated names include the guest name, UTC timestamp and job ID. The archive must be a full Cloudmin virtual machine backup. Other archive formats cannot be restored here.

Cloudmin checks the manifest and member checksums before confirmation and checks the archive again before importing disks. Checksums detect corruption; they do not prove who created an archive. Restore only archives you trust.
