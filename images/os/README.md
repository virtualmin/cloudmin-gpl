# Distribution icons

These SVGs are served locally by Cloudmin and displayed at 16 × 16 pixels.
Custom catalogue entries and unknown distributions use `custom.svg`, drawn
for this module.

- `alma.svg`, `centos.svg`, `debian.svg`, `fedora.svg`, `rocky.svg`, `suse.svg`
  and `ubuntu.svg` come from Authentic Theme's `images/modules/servers`
  directory. Its MIT notice is in `LICENSE.authentic-theme`.
- `alpine.svg` and `arch.svg` come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons),
  with their distribution colors applied. The CC0 notice is in
  `LICENSE.simple-icons`.

Distribution names and logos remain the property of their respective owners.
